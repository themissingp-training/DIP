The rubric

Three things we score. Three things we build.

01 · Content

Design that holds together

Turn expertise into a session with a clear purpose, a structure and objectives you can test.

  • Specific purpose & central idea
  • Templates for short and full-day sessions
  • Assessments and evaluation forms
02 · Engagement

A room that leans in

Hook the audience early and keep them working, even on technical topics.

  • STAR hook, gap theory, WIIFM
  • PACE Elements
  • Role plays and simulations
03 · Delivery

Presence on purpose

Voice, body and composure that make people trust the trainer.

  • Pause, pace, volume, pitch
  • Posture, gesture, movement
  • Handling resistance and interruptions
